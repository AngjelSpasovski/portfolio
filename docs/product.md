# Product Direction

## Purpose

The portfolio is a professional evidence base for software-engineering applications. It should present Angjel as a senior frontend/product engineer with long-running enterprise experience, practical public products, and clear ownership of delivered work.

## Primary Audience

- engineering managers and technical interviewers
- recruiters evaluating frontend and product-engineering roles
- potential clients or collaborators reviewing public work

## Core Message

Reliable frontend engineering for complex products, supported by real project evidence rather than a long list of keywords.

## Product Principles

- Show fewer, stronger projects with context, role, contribution, and outcome.
- Distinguish personal products, team-based enterprise work, client work, and learning projects.
- Use accurate claims. Do not imply full ownership of team products.
- Treat privacy and permission as release requirements for screenshots.
- Keep English and Macedonian equivalent in meaning, but naturally written in each language.
- Keep the public experience fast, accessible, responsive, and usable without a backend.
- Prefer verifiable evidence: live links, public repositories, credential URLs, and sanitized visuals.

## Current Product Areas

- hero and professional summary
- about and career facts
- work experience
- technical capabilities
- selected projects: DBStore, Opera MES, and DentCare Macedonia
- certifications
- contact and downloadable CV
- dark and light themes
- English and Macedonian routes
- localized project case-study detail views
- one canonical content source shared by the website and CV
- automated lint, end-to-end, and build checks before deployment

## Planned Product Direction

- project categories and filters when the catalogue is large enough
- a separate Project Hub entry for learning work, after a dedicated integration review

## Explicit Non-Goals For Now

- backend, database, authentication, or browser-based admin
- publishing every small exercise as a flagship project
- duplicating the complete Experience section in another timeline component
- presenting private enterprise screenshots without deliberate sanitization
- adding technologies only for search keywords
